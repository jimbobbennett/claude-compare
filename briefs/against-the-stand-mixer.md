## Terms to define
- Planetary mixing action — beater spins on its own axis while orbiting the bowl
- Tilt-head vs bowl-lift — head hinges back vs bowl raises on arms; affects cabinet clearance
- Worm gear — nylon drive gear engineered to strip first and protect the metal gear train
- Power hub — front attachment port that drives pasta rollers, grinders, spiralizers
- Gluten development — protein network built by kneading or by time; the thing the mixer automates
- No-knead method — long slow fermentation substituting for mechanical kneading
- Hydration — water weight as a percentage of flour weight; drives how hard a dough is to mix
- Bowl capacity (quarts) — nominal volume, not usable volume, given fill limits
- Soft/firm/stiff peaks — egg-white aeration stages used as a mixer benchmark
- Sacrificial part — component intentionally designed to fail to protect costlier ones
