## Key facts
- Newzoo's 2024 industry report found 57% of console/PC playtime went to games six or more years old, with only 12% on new releases (https://www.gamefile.news/p/newzoo-new-games-playing-time-xbox-boycott-neowiz-lies-of-p)
- On PC specifically, 67% of 2024 playtime went to games at least six years old; Counter-Strike 2/GO, League of Legends, Roblox and Dota led (https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/two-thirds-of-total-pc-playtime-last-year-was-on-games-that-are-at-least-six-years-old-report-says/)
- UK Competition and Markets Authority data: AAA budgets moved from $50–150m to $200m+ for 2024–25 releases, with GTA and Call of Duty franchises cited above $250m and $300m (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%932025_video_game_industry_layoffs)
- Ubisoft reported a €159m loss for FY2024-25 and a 20.5% drop in net bookings, despite Assassin's Creed Shadows selling well (https://gamerant.com/ubisoft-financial-report-losses-2025/)
- Ubisoft CEO Yves Guillemot described FY2024-25 as "challenging" with "mixed dynamics across our portfolio" in the official earnings release (https://staticctf.ubisoft.com/8aefmxkxpxwl/75aGUbWM9kwnQj44Tr7d6H/ce4ca047ddadf45cf1e98cea8d1d7a78/Ubisoft_FY25_PR_English_vfinal.pdf)
- Assassin's Creed Valhalla main story clocks ~61 hours on HowLongToBeat; a Ubisoft dev later conceded the game's accumulated content became "messy" (https://www.thegamer.com/assassins-creed-ac-assassins-creed-valhalla-game-size-length-ubisoft/)
- Assassin's Creed Shadows was deliberately scaled back to a 30–40 hour critical path, ~80 hours for full completion (https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2025/03/assassins-creed-shadows-length-between-40-and-80-hours-new-gameplus-under-consideration)
- Ubisoft publicly acknowledged length complaints as far back as Valhalla's marketing, promising it would not be "the longest or biggest game in the series" (https://www.gamespot.com/articles/assassins-creed-valhalla-wont-be-as-long-as-previo/1100-6476984/)
- The "Ubisoft formula" is defined in coverage as towers revealing map regions, checklists of activities, outpost clearing, and icon-saturated maps (https://gamerant.com/star-wars-ubisoft-open-world/)
- Elden Ring deliberately omitted pre-placed question-mark points of interest, requiring players to set their own markers (https://www.resetera.com/threads/new-elden-ring-interviews-with-hidetaka-miyazaki-fromsofts-largest-most-free-form-map.442033/page-5)
- Miyazaki stated the open-world design aimed to avoid the commonly criticised problems of the genre, including emptiness and balance (https://blog.playstation.com/2022/01/28/an-interview-with-fromsoftwares-hidetaka-miyazki/)
- Starfield shipped with 1,000+ explorable planets built largely on procedural generation, with Bethesda arguing handcrafted content exceeded Skyrim and Fallout 4 combined (https://www.pcgamer.com/todd-howard-says-starfields-1000-planets-wont-be-all-boring-procgen-globes-and-contain-more-handcrafted-work-than-skyrim-and-fallout-4-combined/)
- Ex-Rockstar designer Cameron Williams addressed "open world fatigue" in a GDC panel, framing it as players not exploring and being unable to pick games up and put them down (https://www.gamereactor.eu/former-gta-vi-and-rdr2-developer-explains-open-world-fatigue-1518473/)
- Final Fantasy 7 Remake series director Naoki Hamaguchi has publicly accepted open-world fatigue as real, citing criticism of Rebirth's open world (https://www.gamesradar.com/games/final-fantasy/final-fantasy-7-revelation-director-says-open-world-fatigue-is-real-and-since-the-world-is-even-bigger-than-it-was-in-rebirth-square-enix-specially-focused-on-guiding-players/)
- Independent Steam achievement analysis of 19 single-player games found a median completion rate of 36%, closer to one in four for large RPGs (https://www.twoaveragegamers.com/game-completion-rates/)

## Terms to define
- Open world — game structure where the player traverses a continuous, freely navigable map rather than discrete levels
- Ubisoft formula / tower mechanic — synchronisation point that reveals map region and populates it with activity icons
- Procedural generation — content (terrain, quests, layouts) generated algorithmically at runtime or build time rather than hand-authored
- Handcrafted content — locations and encounters individually designed by humans; the counterpart to procgen
- Critical path — the mainline story sequence, excluding optional side content
- HowLongToBeat — crowdsourced database of player-reported completion times, widely cited as a length benchmark
- Net bookings — publisher metric combining digital, physical and subscription revenue recognised in a period
- AAA — high-budget, high-marketing production tier; CMA now benchmarks this at $200m+ development
- Live service — continuously updated, monetised game designed for indefinite retention rather than completion
- Completion rate — share of owners unlocking an end-of-game achievement; a proxy floor for how many players finish
- Content padding — repeated low-authorship activities used to extend playtime
- Possibility space — the set of actions and destinations available to a player at a given moment
