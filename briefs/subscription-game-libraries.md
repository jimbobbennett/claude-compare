## Key facts
- Microsoft raised Xbox Game Pass Ultimate from $19.99 to $29.99/month, a 50% increase, announced and effective immediately on Oct 1, 2025 (https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/01/microsoft-price-hike-xbox-game-pass-ultimate.html)
- Essential and Premium tiers kept their prices in the same restructure; only Ultimate rose (https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/01/microsoft-price-hike-xbox-game-pass-ultimate.html)
- Ultimate bundling now folds in third-party subscriptions — Ubisoft+ Classics and Fortnite Crew — inside one fee (https://www.thegamebusiness.com/p/xbox-game-pass-ultimate-price-jumps)
- Microsoft's last officially confirmed Game Pass subscriber figure was February 2024; no official count published since (https://www.statista.com/statistics/1276183/xbox-game-pass-subscriber-count-global/)
- Press reporting (WSJ-sourced) puts current Game Pass at roughly 30 million against an internal target of 77 million by 2026 — unconfirmed by Microsoft, treat as estimate (https://www.notebookcheck.net/Xbox-Game-Pass-has-30-million-subscribers-after-price-cut-well-short-of-77-mil-goal.1336022.0.html)
- Sony reported 47.0 million PlayStation Plus subscribers at the close of FY2025, against ~129 million PSN monthly active users — subscription penetration is a minority of the installed base (https://tech-insider.org/ps5-93-million-sony-record-profit-2026/)
- Sony has publicly stated its strategy is funnelling existing players into higher-priced PS Plus tiers rather than only adding new subscribers (https://gamerant.com/sony-ps-plus-profits-statement/)
- Catalog rotation is structural, not incidental: third-party titles typically enter on 12–24 month licensing deals and leave when terms lapse (https://geekchamp.com/all-games-leaving-game-pass-this-month-february-2026/)
- First-party titles rarely leave Game Pass; third-party titles rotate out monthly — 17 games removed in February 2025 alone (https://www.gamespot.com/articles/why-does-game-pass-remove-games/1100-6529844/)
- Same title can leave and return depending on renegotiation — GTA 5 re-entered Game Pass April 2025 after earlier departure (https://gameluster.com/what-games-are-coming-off-game-pass-in-2026-full-r/)
- California AB 2426 took effect Jan 1, 2025, barring sellers from using "buy" or "purchase" for digital goods without disclosing that the customer receives only a licence (https://www.wsgr.com/en/insights/ab-2426-californias-new-law-strengthens-protections-against-false-or-misleading-advertisement-claims-regarding-digital-goods.html)
- AB 2426 explicitly covers digital games and subscription-based access services, amending California's False Advertising Law via new §17500.6 (https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2024/12/ab-2426-new-california-law-requires-clear-licensing-disclosures-for-digital-goods)
- Valve added a licence disclaimer to the Steam shopping cart in October 2024, ahead of AB 2426 — i.e. buying and subscribing converge legally as licensed access (https://www.engadget.com/gaming/steam-now-tells-gamers-up-front-that-theyre-buying-a-license-not-a-game-085106522.html)
- The "Stop Killing Games" European Citizens' Initiative passed 1.4 million signatures in 2025; once validated, the European Commission must formally respond within six months (https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/game-development/stop-killing-games-eu-initiative-hits-1-4-million-signatures-and-if-at-least-1-million-are-valid-its-off-to-the-european-commission/)
- Sony's digital share of full-game purchases reached 76% in FY2024 and 85% by Q4 FY2025 — subscription shift sits on top of an already near-total digital transition (https://icon-era.com/statistics/sony-interactive-entertainment/)

## Terms to define
- Day-one release — title added to a subscription catalog on its retail launch date rather than later
- Catalog rotation — scheduled removal of titles when licensing terms expire
- Licence vs ownership — right to access under conditions, revocable, non-transferable; distinct from owning a copy
- Tier — pricing band within one service (e.g. Essential/Premium/Ultimate) with differing catalog, cloud and online-play rights
- ARPU (average revenue per user) — revenue divided by active users; the metric tier-upgrade strategies target
- MAU (monthly active users) — platform-wide active accounts, distinct from paying subscribers
- Cloud streaming — running the game on remote servers, video sent to the device; no local install
- European Citizens' Initiative (ECI) — EU petition mechanism obliging a formal Commission response at 1m validated signatures
- Delisting — permanent removal of a title from a storefront, separate from subscription rotation
- Sell-through — retail units actually sold to consumers, versus units shipped to retailers
