## Key facts
- Passport holders of some countries and regions do not need a visa for stays of 90 days or less when engaging only in tourism activities; JNTO directs travellers to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs site for current rules, which are subject to change at any time (https://www.japan.travel/en/plan/visa-info/)
- Visit Japan Web generates a QR code usable at the immigration counter and at the customs electronic declaration terminal or inspection table (https://services.digital.go.jp/en/visit-japan-web/guide/)
- At Haneda, Fukuoka and Naha airports, the customs declaration can be completed electronically at a terminal, enabling use of the electronic declaration gate (https://services.digital.go.jp/en/visit-japan-web/guide/)
- Japan has officially adopted the name JESTA (Japan Electronic System for Travel Authorization) for a pre-travel clearance system applying to nationals of visa-exempt countries, modelled on the US ESTA (https://visasnews.com/en/japan-officializes-jesta-its-electronic-travel-authorization-system/)
- JESTA launch targeted for fiscal 2028; development plan includes at least 17 months design and development, ~8 months testing with related systems, 3 months staff training (https://visasnews.com/en/japans-parliament-unveils-new-details-on-jesta-the-electronic-travel-authorization-planned-for-2028/)
- Japan Rail Pass is sold in 7, 14 and 21 consecutive-day durations, in Standard and Green Car classes, purchasable on the official reservation website or an authorised agency outside Japan (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Rail_Pass)
- Passes bought online require the traveller to select the validity start date at purchase and collect the physical pass at a dedicated ticket office (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Rail_Pass)
- Welcome Suica, the tourist IC card, has been sold since 27 March 2025 at a defined list of stations including Narita Terminal 1, Narita Terminals 2·3, Haneda Terminal 3 monorail, Tokyo, Shibuya, Shinjuku, Ikebukuro, Ueno, Yokohama and Sendai; in principle one card per person (https://www.jreast.co.jp/en/multi/welcomesuica/purchase.html)
- Blank standard Suica cards returned to unrestricted sale in March 2025 after a pause caused by the global chip shortage (https://tokyocheapo.com/travel/suica-card-guide/)
- Japan's cashless payment ratio was 42.8% of consumer payments in 2024, meaning most transaction value is still non-cashless (https://www.meti.go.jp/english/press/2025/0331_001.html)
- Kyoto's accommodation tax is restructured from 1 March 2026, replacing the ¥200–¥1,000 per-night bands with tiers rising to ¥10,000 per person per night at the top (https://www.travelpulse.com/news/destinations/popular-japanese-travel-destination-kyoto-raising-tourist-tax)
- Travellers who booked Kyoto accommodation before the announcement but stay after 1 March 2026 pay the new higher rates (https://japanspecialist.com/w/kyoto-hotel-tax-to-increase-up-to-900-percent-in-2026)
- Mount Fuji trails now charge an admission fee with advance online reservation and payment requested; same-day purchase possible at the trailhead (https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e6901.html)
- Yamanashi doubled its Mount Fuji fee on 3 March 2025; Shizuoka imposed a trailhead access fee on the south side for the first time (https://www.stripes.com/theaters/asia_pacific/2025-03-19/mount-fuji-climbing-fees-restrictions-17201660.html)

## Terms to define
- **JNTO** — Japan National Tourism Organization; the government body that publishes official arrival statistics and traveller guidance
- **Visit Japan Web** — Digital Agency web service that pre-registers immigration and customs data and issues a scannable QR code
- **JESTA** — planned Japan Electronic System for Travel Authorization; pre-departure online clearance for visa-exempt nationals
- **Exchange Order** — voucher issued when a JR Pass is bought abroad, swapped for the physical pass at a designated ticket office in Japan
- **Green Car** — first-class carriage on JR trains; a separate, higher JR Pass tier
- **IC card** — prepaid contactless card (Suica, Pasmo, ICOCA) for transit fares and small retail purchases
- **Welcome Suica** — short-validity tourist IC card sold at limited airport and station locations, one per person
- **Accommodation tax** — municipal per-person, per-night levy on lodging, charged on top of the room rate; tiered by room price
- **Code payment** — QR/barcode smartphone payment (e.g. PayPay), tracked separately from cards in METI's cashless figures
- **Trailhead access fee** — per-climber charge levied by prefectural government for entry to a mountain route, separate from any transport or hut cost
