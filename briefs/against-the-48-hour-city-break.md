## Key facts
- 56.3% of tourism trips made by EU residents in 2024 lasted a maximum of three overnight stays; trips of 4+ nights were 43.7% (https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Tourism_statistics_-_characteristics_of_tourism_trips)
- In Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Finland and Spain, more than two in three trips by residents were short trips of ≤3 nights (https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Tourism_statistics_-_characteristics_of_tourism_trips)
- Short-haul aviation is more carbon-intensive per kilometre than long-haul because take-off and landing consume disproportionate fuel — so the flight-to-holiday ratio worsens as trips shorten (https://www.robeco.com/en-int/insights/2020/02/short-haul-flights-are-the-worst-offenders-for-co)
- Worst-performing flights on a fuel-per-passenger-km basis are trips of roughly 500–1,500 km — precisely the city-break band — partly due to less efficient regional jets (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuel_economy_in_aircraft)
- Average length of stay in Destination Barcelona fell from 2.9 nights (2024) to 2.6 nights (2025); hotel stays averaged 2.2 nights (https://observatoriturisme.barcelona/en/noticia/la-despesa-durant-lestada-creix-a-la-destinacio-barcelona-fins-a-gairebe-els-90e-per-persona-i-dia/)
- Academic expenditure study of Amsterdam city trips: tourists spend less per person per day the longer they stay overnight — short stays front-load high-intensity consumption (https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s10824-017-9293-1.pdf)
- Venice introduced a €5 day-tripper access fee on 29 peak days in 2024, extended to 54 days in 2025, with last-minute bookers charged €10 (https://traveltomorrow.com/venice-considers-raising-day-tripper-fee-to-e30-as-overtourism-persists/)
- Venice's access fee trial ended 27 July 2026; the fee no longer applies and future application rests with the city's governing bodies (https://cda.ve.it/en/)
- Overnight visitors to Venice pay a separate tourist tax of €1–€5 per person per night, i.e. the short-stay visitor is taxed twice over under overlapping schemes (https://traveltomorrow.com/venice-considers-raising-day-tripper-fee-to-e30-as-overtourism-persists/)
- Of Venice's roughly 20 million annual visitors, more than 20 million visit-instances involve less than a full day in the city (https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/venices-first-of-its-kind-day-tripper-tax-sparks-outrage-180984258/)
- Amsterdam city council policy: cap of 20 million annual overnight stays, ban on new hotels (one-in, one-out), and reduction of river cruise arrivals (https://karryon.com.au/forgood/amsterdam-overtourism-ban/)
- Amsterdam's "Stay Away" discouragement campaign widened in 2024 to EU and Dutch audiences, with a pop-up questionnaire screening prospective visitors' motives (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stay_Away_(advertisement))
- Louvre crowd management: queue can start under the glass pyramid, hundreds of yards from the painting, with roughly one minute at the front before visitors are moved on (https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/louvre-visitors-are-angry-over-increasingly-brief-encounters-mona-lisa-180972908/)
- Slow-travel literature defines the counter-model: travel less often, stay longer, favour surface transport, treat the journey as part of the experience (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7798061/)
- Jet lag guidance commonly cited as roughly one day of adjustment per time zone crossed — longer than a 48-hour trip in most long-haul cases (https://www.hotelsbyday.com/blog/recover-after-travel-jet-lag-burnout/)

## Terms to define
- Overtourism — visitor volumes exceeding a destination's carrying capacity for residents, infrastructure or heritage
- Day-tripper / excursionist — visitor who does not stay overnight; counted separately from tourists in official statistics
- Contributo di accesso — Venice's per-day access fee for non-overnight visitors, enforced via advance QR-code booking
- LTO cycle — landing and take-off phase of a flight; disproportionately fuel-hungry, penalising short routes
- Average length of stay — mean nights per visitor; standard destination-management metric
- Bed-night / overnight stay — one person spending one night in registered accommodation; unit for caps and tourist taxes
- Slow travel — travel model favouring fewer, longer trips and surface transport over frequent short-haul flying
- Carrying capacity — maximum visitor load a site can absorb without degrading experience or asset
- Tourism recommender system (TRS) — algorithmic destination-suggestion tools, now being modelled with CO₂e and seasonality inputs
- Undertourism — inverse condition: destinations left underused through poor access, infrastructure or visibility
